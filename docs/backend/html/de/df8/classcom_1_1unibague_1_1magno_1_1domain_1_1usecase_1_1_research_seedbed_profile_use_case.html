Detailed Description

Use case implementation for managing research seedbed profiles.

Handles business logic for research seedbed profile operations. A research seedbed profile represents the configuration and state of a research seedbed within a specific academic period, including its coordinator, tutor, and association with an investigation group profile.

Why a helper is used: This use case delegates complex operations to IResearchSeedbedProfileHelper to avoid circular dependencies between use cases and to encapsulate the complex logic of managing functionary profiles when coordinators or tutors change. The helper handles creating, updating, or deleting functionary profiles based on their usage across all seedbed profiles in an academic period.

Business rules enforced:

  • A research seedbed can only have one profile per academic period
  • Only current (active) periods allow profile modifications
  • Coordinator and tutor cannot be the same person

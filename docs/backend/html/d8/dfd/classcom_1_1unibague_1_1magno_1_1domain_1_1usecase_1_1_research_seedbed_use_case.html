Detailed Description

Use case implementation for managing research seedbeds.

Handles business logic for research seedbed operations. Research seedbeds are groups within investigation groups where students participate in research activities under the guidance of tutors and coordinators.

Business rules enforced:

  • Seedbed names must be unique (case-insensitive)
  • Seedbeds with associated profiles cannot be deleted
